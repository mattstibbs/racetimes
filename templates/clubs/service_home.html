Race Times

Race results, handicaps and series standings for sailing clubs, run as a service. Each club gets its own site, with its own members, race committee and results.

What a club gets

  • Public results and series standings, on a phone or at the clubhouse, for anyone to see.
  • RYA National Handicap for Cruisers (NHC) handicaps, worked out after every race, with RRS Appendix A scoring.
  • A race day page for the race committee: a start sheet, and a button to record each finish.
  • Results emailed to boat owners, and a change history of every correction.
  • Members register their boats and enter series themselves; the race committee approves.

Sailing at a club that uses it?

Each club has its own address, like yourclub.racetimes.co.uk. Your club will give you its address; that's where you'll find its results, and where you sign up.
