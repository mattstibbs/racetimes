What your club gets

  • Results on every phone

    Race results and series standings for sailors, their families and the clubhouse, as soon as the race committee publishes them.

  • NHC handicaps, worked out for you

    RYA National Handicap for Cruisers adjustments after every race, with Appendix A scoring and discards. No spreadsheets.

  • A race day page for the committee

    A start sheet, a race clock and a Finished button for each boat. Tap as they cross the line, and undo a slip.

  • Members do their own admin

    Owners register their boats and enter series themselves. The race committee approves, and results are emailed to every owner.

  • Every correction on record

    Change a result and give a reason. The history shows who changed what and when, and amended results say so.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your club

    Email us your club's name and who will look after it.

  2. We set up your site

    Your club gets its own address, like yourclub.{{ service_domain }}, and we invite whoever will run it.

  3. Go racing

    Your race committee records the finishes. Handicaps, standings and results emails follow.
