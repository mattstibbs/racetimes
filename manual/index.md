# Race Times user manual

Race Times keeps a sailing club's racing in one place: the boats, the series
and races, the finish times, and the results. It scores every race under the
RYA's National Handicap for Cruisers (NHC) and the Racing Rules of Sailing, so
results and handicaps are worked out for you.

Each club has its own Race Times site, at its own address, such as
`demo.racetimes.co.uk`. Your club's name is at the top of every page, and
everything you see there belongs to your club alone.

This manual explains how to use it. It is written for the people who use the
site, not for developers.

## Who can do what

What you can do depends on your role at the club. The site only ever shows
you the parts you can use. Your role belongs to you at this club: if you sail
at two clubs that both use Race Times, you use one login, and each club gives
you your own role there.

| You are… | You can… |
|---|---|
| **Anyone** | See results and series standings, and sign up or ask to join the club. |
| **A member** | Register your boats, ask for changes to them, ask to be recorded as the owner of a boat already on record, and ask to enter a series. You can see where each request stands, and withdraw it while it is waiting. |
| **The race committee** | Approve or reject members' requests; set up boats, series and races; enter finish times; and see the history of every change to results. |
| **A club administrator** | Everything the race committee can do, and also approve people joining the club, and choose each person's role. |

Members never change anything directly. Everything a member asks for goes to
the race committee, and nothing changes until the committee approves it. That
way every boat, entry and result has been checked by someone on the committee.
